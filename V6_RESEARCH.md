# V6_RESEARCH.md — Candidate upgrades for a v6 / v5.1 PLO5 double-board bomb-pot net

## Orientation

This is a decision-ready catalog of upgrades for the "end-all" PLO5 double-board bomb-pot agent, ranked and grouped by the three critiques that define the objective function: **(1) face-up sizings**, **(2) imperfect decisions**, **(3) over-mixing in check-or-bet spots**, plus general strength/infra and a separate departures section. Every finding was scouted from the RL / imperfect-information-games literature and then adversarially verified against both the source papers and the actual repo code (`ppo.py`, `network.py`, `encoding.py`, `rollout.py`, `rust_engine/`). **Where the scout's optimism and the adversarial verdict disagree, this document sides with the adversary** — several headline "high-gain" findings are downgraded or cut below.

Three things dominate the honest picture and recur throughout:
- **The single most-evidenced, best-fit, checkpoint-safe upgrade is the VRPO / Expected-SARSA(λ) advantage flip** — the machinery is *already staged and zero-init in every v5 checkpoint*, so it's a code change, not a checkpoint break.
- **Multiway is native to bomb pots** (uniform 2–6 seats, verified in `vFive1_guardian.sh`). This *silently voids* the convergence guarantees of an entire cluster of otherwise-attractive equilibrium-magnet papers (NashPG, R-NaD, GARIP) that are proven only for two-player zero-sum. Those get REFUTED as "high-gain" even though they're real, cheap, and axiom-clean — they degrade to unproven heuristics in the exact regime you train.
- **Several obs/aux ideas are already shipped or don't have the labels they assume.** The JOINT two-board outcome block *already exists* at obs offset 978 (`tally_joint`); per-board showdown-share labels *do not exist* at the payout boundary (`double_board_payout` returns a merged scalar). Both facts were verified in-repo and kill findings that looked strong on paper.

### Scoring legend

- **Expected gain** — `high` reserved for mechanism-clear, evidence-backed wins on this exact game; `medium` = plausible/partial; `low` = second-order or preventative. (Adversarially corrected value is used, not the scout's.)
- **Impl cost** — `low` (≈ config/loss-term, hours), `medium` (new head/estimator + A/B stem), `high` (subsystem / rollout-architecture change).
- **Disruption** — `incremental` (knob or additive term), `moderate` (new head / loss / schedule), `departure` (paradigm shift, e.g. inference-time search, learned world model).
- **Axiom fit** — `clean` / `tension` (mechanical friction, resolvable) / `CONFLICT` (violates a hard axiom — flagged, not adopted).
- **Source** — `verified-real` (paper fetched/confirmed) vs `recalled` (design synthesis, no single citation — treat as proposal).
- **Adversarial verdict** — `CONFIRMED` (real + transfers + axiom-clean), `PLAUSIBLE` (real but with a correction/caveat), `REFUTED` (real paper but doesn't transfer, or a load-bearing claim is false). Absent where no verify pass ran.

This is a **research catalog, not a validated benchmark.** Nothing here has been A/B'd on this game. The prerequisite that makes *any* of it measurable — an exploitability probe / eval stack — is itself a finding (see Infra).

## Top 5 — do these first

These five are the highest expected value among the well-verified, axiom-clean findings, weighted toward the three critiques and toward things whose plumbing already exists.

1. **VRPO / Expected-SARSA(λ) advantage flip (finish the staged Q-head).** *What:* replace GAE's V-based advantage with a multi-step Expected-SARSA(λ) trace off the all-cards dueling Q head. *Why:* it removes the future-action-sampling variance that persists **even with a perfect V** — precisely the "variance floor" the design blames for slow/wrong mixed-node frequencies (#2) and the noisy size gradients that let "more scoop equity → bigger bet" dominate (#1). *v5 fit:* the zero-init `adv_head` (`network.py:924`, `q_actions=2+anchor_count`) and the `q_idx` gather (`ppo.py:348-356`) already ship in every vFive checkpoint; Q≡V at load, so flipping the estimator is a code change, not a checkpoint break. **CONFIRMED** (arXiv:2605.19235), gain corrected high→medium: it de-noises the gate + anchor dims (where over-mixing lives) but the Q is refine-blind, so within-anchor sizing still rides V-GAE.

2. **Ganzfried-style purification / thresholding of the served GATE (inference-time, study-tool-only).** *What:* zero sub-τ gate probabilities and renormalize (or argmax) before displaying the recommendation, tuned to an exploitability probe. *Why:* over-mixing (#3) is a gate-entropy problem the K-mixture sizing head is orthogonal to; a softmax gate structurally never reaches pure, and a serving-side threshold snaps pure-where-pure with **zero training risk, zero parity impact**, fully reversible (mirror `PLO5BP_SERVE_EMA`). *v5 fit:* `ActorCriticV2.act(deterministic=True)` already argmaxes the single rec; the delta is a soft-τ knob on the *displayed* gate distribution. **PLAUSIBLE**, gain corrected to low but cost is genuinely low and it's the cleanest direct lever on #3 — ship it as an optional display filter, **default OFF**, and do NOT let it feed training until a probe confirms it doesn't inflate exploitability at genuinely-mixed bluff-catcher nodes.

3. **The exploitability / purity / face-up eval stack (build this FIRST).** *What:* duplicate/CRN paired matches + AIVAT-lite variance reduction reusing the all-cards critic as the baseline + a learned/LBR best-response probe + three per-critique scalars (bet-size↔scoop-equity MI; gate-entropy on solver-pure nodes; bluff-catch call-frequency error). *Why:* every other item on this list is unfalsifiable without it — the design says so repeatedly ("needs an exploitability probe as a success metric"). It's read-only tooling, axiom-clean, no checkpoint/obs change. *v5 fit:* extend `scripts/exploitability.py`; reuse `server._load_critic` + `rollout._rotate_opp_holes` so the baseline matches training. Cheap relative to its unlock.

4. **Pre-activation LayerNorm on the CRITIC torso + weight decay (plasticity, applied where the evidence is strongest).** *What:* insert LayerNorm before each Linear in the residual blocks (`x + ReLU(Linear(LayerNorm(x)))`) and pair it with decoupled weight decay / L2-to-init. *Why:* on-policy PPO continually shifts its own targets — the regime that induces plasticity loss (dead ReLUs, effective-LR decay), letting the net ossify on marginal spots (#2) over a multi-million-step run. *v5 fit:* apply to `CentralCritic.torso` first (the survey's value-network evidence is strongest there and there's no exploitability-generalization downside), then A/B the actor trunk separately. **PLAUSIBLE, corrected medium** — the source (Nauman et al. 2024) finds LayerNorm *alone* can *hurt generalization* and recommends it **combined** with a shrink-perturb/regen companion; do NOT ship LN-solo, and use an exploitability probe as the gate since the risk axis is opponent-generalization.

5. **PFSP / quality-score opponent sampling (replace uniform pool draws).** *What:* sample pool opponents by a win-rate-shaped weight — `f_hard(x)=(1-x)^p` to over-weight opponents beating you, or the cheaper OpenAI-Five scalar-quality softmax with a 20% uniform-history floor — instead of the current uniform draw. *Why:* uniform sampling wastes rollout budget re-beating dominated snapshots and under-trains against the specific past selves still exploiting the current policy, leaving residual exploitability (#2); a hardness curve concentrates data on the live frontier and the 80/20 split is a proven anti-forgetting default the current setup lacks. *v5 fit:* re-weight the snapshot assignment in `collect_rollout_batched`/`_assign_pool_mix`, keeping a running EWMA of hero-vs-snapshot win-rate from terminal rewards; training-loop-only, checkpoints load unchanged, keep a high uniform floor to respect the intentional uniform-2-6-seat coverage.

*(Deliberately excluded from the Top 5 despite scout hype: the NashPG / R-NaD / GARIP "iteratively-refined magnet" cluster — real and cheap, but their sole guarantee is 2-player-zero-sum-only and provably breaks in native-multiway bomb pots; see "Imperfect decisions" for the honest read. And inference-time re-solve — the highest-ceiling idea, but it needs a public-belief-state / range-reconstruction subsystem the critic is not; see Departures.)*

## Face-up sizings (Critique #1)

The design's own verdict: face-up sizing is a **structural + regularization** problem, not a head-expressiveness problem — the size distribution is a pure function of obs and no input feature *forces* strength-independence. The strongest levers are therefore variance reduction (so the "more equity → bigger bet" gradient stops dominating), the kl-anchor/pool pressure, and an explicit balance regularizer. Ranked best-first.

**1. VRPO / Expected-SARSA(λ) advantage flip** *(cross-listed #1 in Top 5; primary home is General strength)*
One-liner: lower-variance advantages sharpen the size gradient at the flop, the most face-up-prone node.
- Source: verified-real — Fan & Farina, "GAE Falls Short in Imperfect-Information Self-Play RL", arXiv:2605.19235.
- Mechanism: Expected-SARSA residual δ⁺ = r + γV^π(s′) − Q(s,a) with V^π = Σₐπ(a)Q(s,a); removes action-sampling variance the scalar-V GAE cannot.
- Disruption: moderate. Expected gain: **medium** (corrected from high — Q is refine-blind, so the *continuous* size dimension where #1 most lives is NOT directly de-noised; the win lands on gate + anchor). Impl cost: medium.
- Axiom fit: clean (CTDE critic, no obs/sizing-head change, warm-start preserved, parity via a golden `Â_boost==GAE when Q≡V` test).
- Adversarial verdict: **CONFIRMED**. Reason: formula/critic requirement/finite-action scope all verified against the paper; matches V5_DESIGN.md W2.5 verbatim. Honest caveat: the +33±19 mBB/h vs Slumbot is single-board/heads-up and the authors call it "not a decisive win"; treat magnitude as directional, not 1:1 to double-board.
- Applies to v5: flip the advantage source in `ppo.py.update()` after `--q-aux-coef>0` warms the head; keep V for returns/UI. The *real v6 lift for #1* is a **refine-conditioned Q** (below) so the flip reaches within-anchor sizing.

**2. Explicit strength-independence (size-balance) regularizer on the sizing marginal**
One-liner: penalize divergence between the anchor-size distribution of strong-scoop hands and bluff/thin hands sharing a betting node, bucketed by *objective* counts.
- Source: recalled-unverified — balanced-range / minimally-exploitable-sizing theory (Libratus bet-size abstraction); no single paper prescribes this exact auxiliary loss. Treat as a design proposal.
- Mechanism: auxiliary term over `_anchor_dist().probs` averaged per objective-strength bucket (e.g. per-board ahead-count or the joint-scoop count) — a symmetry/consistency loss on P(anchor | node, class). Rides the parametric marginal, not free logits.
- Disruption: moderate. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: high (bucketing + coef anneal + probe validation).
- Axiom fit: **tension** — it's a hand-crafted balancing objective (like the kl-anchor magnet, which is also a deliberately-imposed regularizer), and if mis-tuned it fights *genuine* strength-correlated sizing that IS equilibrium-correct (polarization is real). Bucket key MUST be objective counts only.
- Applies to v5: new `ppo.py` loss behind a flag; success metric = the size-leakage probe ("does bet size predict scoop equity") WITHOUT raising overall exploitability. Throwaway-stem A/B first.

**3. Mutual-information penalty I(strength ; size | obs) (privacy-regularized PG)**
One-liner: a variational discriminator on the all-cards critic predicts the sampled anchor from public-obs + size; the actor pays −λ·log q to make size un-predictive of private strength.
- Source: verified-real — Cundy, Desai & Ermon, "Privacy-Constrained Policies via MI-Regularized PG", AISTATS 2024, arXiv:2012.15019.
- Mechanism: objective = E[reward] − λ·I(sensitive; action | obs), MI upper-bounded by a supervised (max-likelihood, not adversarial) predictor. Strength label is an objective count on the critic side, never fed to the actor.
- Disruption: moderate. Expected gain: **low** (corrected from high). Impl cost: high.
- Axiom fit: clean *on the letter* (objective-info-only, parametric-sizing, parity, warm-start all satisfied — discriminator is a zero-init head on `CentralCritic`).
- Adversarial verdict: **PLAUSIBLE**, gain hype. Reasons: (a) the paper is single-agent RL with a *passive* post-hoc adversary — no self-play/multi-agent test, so estimator stability in co-adapting self-play is unestablished; (b) deeper objective conflict — GTO sizing is *legitimately strength-correlated* (value bets bigger, balanced by same-size bluffs); forcing I→0 targets a maximally-disguised, generally non-Nash and −EV strategy, and the paper's own Fig. 4 shows tightening the MI constraint monotonically reduces reward. It pulls toward a different fixed point than the kl-anchor magnet.
- Applies to v5: viable only as a **low-λ ablation** gated behind the kl-anchor magnet trial, with an exploitability probe aborting when reward degrades. Not a standalone v6 pillar.

**4. Refine-conditioned (component/size-aware) all-cards Q head**
One-liner: extend the dueling Q to condition on the sampled within-anchor u (IQN-style embedding or a small continuous-Q branch), so the VRPO advantage reaches the size dimension.
- Source: verified-real — IQN (arXiv:1806.06923), Advantage-Weighted Regression (arXiv:1910.00177); the documented v6 ceiling in the inventory.
- Mechanism: today the Q is anchor-indexed only; conditioning on u yields a lower-variance advantage across the *size* axis, where #1 actually lives.
- Disruption: moderate. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: high (a continuous-action Q is explicitly *out of scope* in the VRPO paper — this is a genuine research lift, not "one more head").
- Axiom fit: clean (CTDE, zero-init so warm-start-safe, actor untouched).
- Applies to v5: the follow-on to Top-5 #1 — do the anchor-level flip first, then this. This is the honest home for "VRPO fixes face-up sizing," which the plain flip does NOT deliver.

**5. Size-axis purification via removal-regret pruning (GTO-Wizard "Dynamic Sizing" analog)**
One-liner: pull a mixture component's weight toward the ε-floor when the Q head says it's low-frequency AND near-zero removal-regret, so menus stay multi-size only where sizes carry real EV.
- Source: verified-real — GTO Wizard "Dynamic Sizing" (iterative frequency/EV/removal-regret pruning + re-solve; ~0.05% pot EV loss on river).
- Mechanism: per-component removal-regret from Q(s, anchor_k) vs the mixture value → a weight-collapse regularizer that is *anti*-mixing, targeted only at redundant components (opposite of the v2 flat-collapse failure).
- Disruption: moderate. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: high (depends on the VRPO Q flip landing first).
- Axiom fit: clean (nudges mixture WEIGHTS only, never independent logits).
- Applies to v5: sequence after W2.5; rides the trained Q head.

**6. Tsallis/sparsemax on the sizing MARGINAL** — *see Over-mixing §, ranked there; medium/medium, clean because it acts on the mixture-derived marginal, not free logits.*

**7. Component-conditional refine head** — *see Departures/architecture; low gain, tension (breaks the exact-marginal-entropy closed form; needs the component index in the buffer).*

**REFUTED / cut (kept for the record):**
- **JOINT two-board outcome counts + blockers-to-scoop as an obs-v3 tail — REFUTED (already in v5).** The finding proposed adding P(scoop)/P(get-scooped)/P(win-one)/P(chop-both) + opp-combos-that-scoop-us. **Verified in-repo: this block already ships** at `encoding.py:198` `_OPP_OUTCOME_OFF=978` (12 dims, the *joint* (cmp_a,cmp_b) pair via `tally_joint`, engine.rs:1370-1378), with per-board win-one/tie-both at offset 991. Every proposed feature maps to an existing dim. Near-zero incremental gain; the only residual is a slightly different framing of blockers-to-scoop vs the existing exhaustive opp-scoop fraction — marginal, not worth a stem.
- **Range-conditioned own-range sizing signal — REFUTED (won't transfer + axiom conflict).** A reach-weighted own-range summary as an actor INPUT is (i) *policy-dependent*, breaking the "obs is a pure function of state" / bit-exact-parity axiom; (ii) computationally a nested per-node range-enumeration loop inside the slab-preallocated rollout (not "an obs block"); (iii) self-referential (uninformative early); (iv) *still doesn't impose balance* — it's just another feature the net can condition size on. The axiom-clean input-side moves (joint counts, blockers-to-scoop) are already present.
- **SISAMS learnable anchor placement — REFUTED (no mechanism here + parity conflict).** SISAMS moves support points by gradient-on-*exploitability* over a metagame; v5 is PPO self-play with no exploitability gradient to move anchors toward, and SISAMS was validated only on abstract auctions/Blotto (zero poker). The within-run learnable-knot version breaks the bit-identical numpy/torch anchor-grid twins; the "safe" fallback (retrain with the ladder nudged toward the served histogram) is a hand-tuned config heuristic, not the cited method, and `anchor_spec_for_count()` keys only on head width so a moved ladder silently reloads as canonical.

## Imperfect decisions (Critique #2)

The dominant lever is variance reduction at the interior (bluff-catch) nodes; the second is a better equilibrium-convergence target. **Read this section with the multiway caveat front and center:** the whole cluster of "iteratively-refined magnet" papers is proven two-player-zero-sum only and does *not* inherit its guarantee in native-multiway bomb pots.

**1. VRPO / Expected-SARSA(λ) advantage flip** — *see Top 5 #1 and Face-up §1. CONFIRMED, medium, medium. The single best-evidenced #2 lever; plumbing already in the checkpoint.*

**2. Drop/soften value-function CLIPPING; rebalance the double-weighted critic loss**
One-liner: two large ablations find value-loss clipping doesn't help (Andrychowicz: "even hurts"); v5 both clips value (0.2 RAW bb) AND ~0.25×-weights it, under-fitting the very all-cards value the VRPO flip needs.
- Source: verified-real — Andrychowicz et al. "What Matters in On-Policy RL?" (C13); Engstrom et al. "Implementation Matters"; "37 Details of PPO".
- Mechanism: remove the clip (unclipped Huber/MSE) and lift the effective critic weight so the centralized critic tracks sharp scoop/freeroll value discontinuities.
- Disruption: incremental. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: low.
- Axiom fit: clean (loss-only, config knob already exists; note vFive1 already loosened to value-clip 10). Unblocks the two Q-based findings.
- Applies to v5: allow `value_clip=0` and decouple the implicit 0.25×; A/B on a throwaway stem per V5_DESIGN B4.

**3. Expected-SARSA(λ) V-target for the actor's own value head (variance-reduced GAE *before* the full flip)**
One-liner: keep V-based advantages but bootstrap with Σₐπ(a)Q(s′,a) instead of the sampled next-state V — a cheaper stepping-stone to the full VRPO flip.
- Source: verified-real — Expected SARSA (van Seijen 2009) generalized in VRPO §3; Retrace(λ) (Munos 2016).
- Mechanism: strictly-lower-variance, unbiased-at-convergence backup; no advantage-recompute loop.
- Disruption: incremental. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: low.
- Axiom fit: clean. Applies to v5: minimal `_flush_trajectory` edit once the Q head is trained; validates the variance-reduction claim on this game before committing the full estimator.

**4. Higher-entropy IIG regime + current-policy self-play curriculum (external validation of v5's high seeds)**
One-liner: a 7,000-run exact-exploitability study finds PPO needs entropy 0.05–0.2 (not library-default 0–0.01), and current-policy self-play beats checkpoint/pool self-play under a fixed budget.
- Source: verified-real — Rudolph et al. arXiv:2502.08938; "Self-Play RL in Big 2" arXiv:2605.28863.
- Mechanism: validates v5's 0.45 seed / 0.10–0.18 floors as correct-directioned; cautions that pool-vs-old-snapshots may be a *weaker* finite-budget curriculum than mixing in more current iterates.
- Disruption: incremental. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: low.
- Axiom fit: clean. Applies to v5: bias the opponent mix toward current/near-current snapshots (pairs with PFSP, Top-5 #5); do NOT anneal entropy below ~0.05 (this study makes the floor a hard constraint, not a free parameter).
- Note: this is the honest read behind the "iterated magnet" hype — the paper found R-NaD *underperformed* generic PG, and plain PPO/MMD matched or beat CFR/PSRO-based DRL. Your existing MMD magnet is already in the competitive family.

**5. Reverse-KL magnet (MMD-faithful) instead of forward-KL**
One-liner: v5's magnet minimizes *forward* KL(current‖ref) which is mean-seeking and *resists* purification; the actual MMD update uses *reverse* KL (mode-seeking) — which is what gives MMD its purification pressure and last-iterate behavior.
- Source: verified-real — Sokota et al. MMD (ICLR 2023); Rudolph et al. arXiv:2502.08938 (verified: "MMD employs reverse KL").
- Mechanism: forward KL penalizes dropping a low-prob action the reference still plays (anti-purification); reverse KL tolerates zeroing it (pro-purification) while keeping the convergence role.
- Disruption: incremental. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: low.
- Axiom fit: clean (one-term change in `_kl_to_reference`; reuse the pre-update snapshot already cloned for `kl_hard`; add an optional proximal −KL(π,π_prev)).
- Applies to v5: `--magnet-reverse-kl` flag; also aligns the implementation with the theory the design cites. Cheap A/B; directly targets #3 as well as #2.

**6. Trinal-Clip PPO — chip-bounded value clipping using fold-values**
One-liner: clip the value target to the game-theoretic chip bounds (value-if-hero-folds, value-if-opp-folds) plus a negative-advantage policy clip — both cut PPO variance in high-variance poker payoffs.
- Source: verified-real — AlphaHoldem (AAAI 2022) Trinal-Clip.
- Mechanism: you can never lose more than you've committed nor win more than the opponent has; a fixed ±0.2bb clip misses the pot-sized-all-in tail. Bounds are *exact objective chip facts* from the engine.
- Disruption: incremental. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: low.
- Axiom fit: clean (bounds from `street_commit`/pot; deterministic; add a backward-compat unit test). Applies to v5: replace the scalar `value_clip` with per-sample committed-chip bounds; the negative-adv clip targets the same heavy-tailed anchor ratios `target_kl` guards.

**7. Action-dependent (per-head-factorized) baseline** — *PLAUSIBLE, medium. This is the same direction as VRPO but the finding cites the weaker single-step Wu-2018 baseline (refuted for variance by Tucker-2018 "Mirage of Action-Dependent Baselines"); implement the multi-step VRPO Expected-SARSA(λ) trace instead. Folded into VRPO above.*

**8. Higher Adam β2 / AdaGC per-tensor clip / CISPO truncated-IS** — *three cheap, independent angles on the documented heavy-tailed anchor-ratio spikes that drive gate collapse (why `target_kl`/`kl_hard` exist). All optimizer-side, clean, low-cost; see Optimization. Sweep β2∈{0.98,0.99,0.999} first (trivial).*

**REFUTED / cut (the "iterated magnet" cluster — real, cheap, axiom-clean, but multiway-void):**
- **NashPG / IMMD — iteratively-refined KL reference (hard-refresh, fixed coef).** *(appears in the findings 3× under slightly different framings)* The paper (arXiv:2510.18183) is real and the mechanism is correctly stated (hold ρ fixed, converge, snap ρ←π, fixed α≈0.2, NOT anneal-to-zero). **PLAUSIBLE at best, REFUTED as "high-gain":** (a) its convergence theorem is **two-player-zero-sum only** — Lemma 2's saddle-point cancellation "vanishes in general-sum," and bomb pots are modally multiway; (b) the "+0.105±0.021 vs MMD" figure some framings cite is **fabricated** — the paper reports Elo-only, no such margin; (c) the paper's own baseline is a *static uniform* reference, so it does NOT demonstrate hard-snap beats a slow EMA (which is what v5 runs); (d) `model_ema` is dual-purpose (magnet ref AND smooth-serving actor) so a faithful hard-snap needs *two* references, more than the claimed "~5 lines." **Defensible only as a low-gain side-stem A/B on the heads-up NLH stem** (where the theory applies), never a PLO5 v6 pillar.
- **R-NaD two-sided reward transform.** Real (DeepNash, Science 2022), but (a) the finding mis-states it — DeepNash keeps η *fixed*, convergence comes from reference *resets* not η-annealing, and the transform is *two-sided* (needs the opponent term) with a *NeuRD/v-trace* inner optimizer, none of which the proposal actually implements; (b) same 2p0s-only guarantee, void multiway; (c) the direct study (2502.08938) found R-NaD *underperformed* generic PG. **REFUTED as high-gain.** Cheap reference-reset experiment only.
- **GARIP running-average / Halpern moving reference.** Real (arXiv:2606.22688) and the *average iterate* is genuinely the object with the 2p0s convergence guarantee — but same multiway void, and its actionable half ("serve the arithmetic time-average, not the EMA") is better captured by the standalone **serve-the-average** infra item below. Not high-gain as a training change.

## Over-mixing / check-or-bet (Critique #3)

This is a **gate-entropy / purification** problem; the K-mixture head is a *sizing* head and is orthogonal. The pure-check region is *larger* than single-board (scarce fold equity), so the net over-bets it. Ranked by directness and safety.

**1. Ganzfried purification / thresholding of the served gate (inference-time)** — *Top 5 #2. PLAUSIBLE, low gain but low cost, cleanest direct #3 lever. Serving-side display filter, default OFF, tuned to a probe; the paper's own caveat is that argmax can be WORSE than a tuned intermediate τ and that its documented win is vs *unadaptive* opponents (it may be *more* exploitable in the full game). So this is a study-display convenience, not an exploitability guarantee.*

**2. Reverse-KL magnet** — *see Imperfect §5. Turns the magnet from an anti-purification headwind into a purification ally; medium/low, clean.*

**3. Purified / hardened magnet REFERENCE (training-time analog of #1)**
One-liner: threshold/argmax the EMA reference's *gate* before taking the KL, so the magnet actively pulls the live policy toward pure gates instead of toward its own over-mixed copy.
- Source: verified-real ingredients — Ganzfried purification + MMD reference regularization; the composition is the concrete instantiation the design's grounding pass asked for.
- Mechanism: purify only the gate term of `_kl_to_reference` (leave anchor/refine KL on the raw reference to protect the parametric head); shifts the fixed point toward pure gates.
- Disruption: incremental. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: low (~10 lines on the reference-side probs; reference is already transient no_grad).
- Axiom fit: clean. Applies to v5: keep τ MILD so genuinely-mixed one-board bluff-catchers survive (over-purifying them RAISES exploitability — Ganzfried's intermediate-threshold caveat); validate with the probe. Composes with reverse-KL.

**4. Sparse Tsallis-entropy (q=2 / sparsemax) regularizer on the GATE**
One-liner: regularize the 3-way gate with Tsallis-q entropy so the optimal gate is a *sparsemax* that assigns EXACT zero to dominated actions — pure where equilibrium is pure, mixed only among near-indifferent actions.
- Source: verified-real — Lee et al. "Sparse MDPs / Causal Sparse Tsallis" (RA-L 2018, arXiv:1709.06293); PCL-Tsallis (arXiv:1802.03501).
- Mechanism: Shannon-entropy softmax structurally can never be pure; Tsallis-q admits exact zeros, so "pure check" becomes representable and reachable, not asymptotically approached. Keeps >1 action when values are close.
- Disruption: moderate. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: medium.
- Axiom fit: **tension** — must guarantee the sampler never draws a zeroed action (sample from the sparse support) and keep `act==evaluate` byte-identical; a gate action at exactly 0 makes its PPO importance ratio undefined if resampled. Scope strictly to the GATE (sizing marginal stays Shannon). Applies to v5: swap the gate entropy term in `evaluate()`/`ppo.py`; A/B the purity metric on known-pure-check classes.

**5. Sparse Tsallis/sparsemax on the sizing MARGINAL** *(cross-listed to Face-up)*
One-liner: apply a Tsallis-q term to the *anchor marginal* so tiny-mass anchors go to exact zero — crisper "solver menu" instead of a smeared logistic tail.
- Source: verified-real — Lee et al. arXiv:1903.00725; "Tsallis RL" arXiv:1902.00137.
- Mechanism: penalizes intermediate anchor probabilities; acts on the *mixture-derived* marginal, never per-anchor free logits, so the v2/v3 flat-collapse pathology is not reintroduced.
- Disruption: moderate. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: medium.
- Axiom fit: clean. Applies to v5: cheapest form swaps the anchor-entropy term to Tsallis-q (preserves the exact closed-form log-prob); keep the ε weight FLOOR on the components (sparsity on the marginal, not the components). NOTE: this is the axiom-CLEAN place to put sparsity — see the CONFLICT entry below for where it is forbidden.

**6. Asymmetric / decoupled clip (Clip-Higher) on the gate**
One-liner: split ε into ε_low<ε_high on the gate log-prob so a suppressed-but-correct pure action regains probability faster (anti-entropy-collapse), then tighten late to purify.
- Source: verified-real — DAPO (arXiv:2503.14476) "Clip-Higher" + entropy-collapse analysis of ε=0.2.
- Mechanism: symmetric clip disproportionately caps low-prob actions' upward updates → premature commitment (the over-betting failure). Decoupled clip keeps the gate two-sided long enough to discover the narrow scoop-weighted bet range.
- Disruption: incremental. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: low.
- Axiom fit: clean (per-head so the sizing menu isn't over-loosened; loss-only). Applies to v5: `clip_low`/`clip_high` in `_clipped_surrogate`, exposable via `anneal_control.json`; tune jointly with `target_kl` (opposite pressures). A/B on a throwaway stem.

**7. Sparse gate distribution via α-entmax head (learnable α)** — *PLAUSIBLE but heavy; **do NOT ship the naive form.** The exact-zeros property is a PPO hazard: an action zeroed by the update after being sampled under the old policy gives log_prob=−∞ → NaN ratio → `kl_hard` discards the whole update, firing precisely under the purification pressure it's meant to create. Multiple integration claims in the finding are false (the deep-spin lib is PyTorch-only so the proposed numpy parity twin can't exist; the magnet's gate KL is softmax-closed-form from logits, not manual-over-probs, so entmax logits would regularize toward a mismatched softmax gate; the swap touches ~9 sites not one). If ever pursued: fixed α=1.5 deterministic `entmax15` + an explicit probability floor (which partly defeats the point) + rewrite the magnet gate-KL. **REFUTED as a clean drop-in; the entropy-anneal + KL-magnet + inference-time purification route is strictly safer.***

**REFUTED / cut:**
- **Applying sparsemax/entmax to the sizing anchor marginal via FREE anchor logits — CONFLICT (hard axiom).** Documented explicitly so the tempting "just sparsify the anchors" idea is routed correctly: sparsemax over *free* anchor logits IS the flat-categorical family the project banned after v2/v3 never stabilized. The axiom-clean version is Tsallis-q on the *mixture-derived* marginal (§5 above), never independent logits.
- **NeuRD / hedging logit update on the gate — kept as research-only (tension).** Real (arXiv:1906.00190, softmax-CFR-equivalent, no-regret) and the right idea for gate purification in principle, but it changes the gradient estimator form (byte-identical v5 resume not preserved) and its interaction with the ratio-clip/kl-anchor needs the capped-IX variant. Gate-only scope; A/B behind an exploitability probe. Not a first move.

## General strength & infrastructure

Levers that raise overall play, plus the measurement stack without which none of the above is falsifiable.

**1. Duplicate/CRN + AIVAT-lite + trained-exploiter/LBR eval stack** — *Top 5 #3. The load-bearing infra prerequisite.*
- Source: verified-real — AIVAT (AAAI 2018, arXiv:1612.06915, >10× fewer hands / ~68% stddev drop); duplicate/CRN (Glasserman & Yao); Approximate Exploitability (Timbers, IJCAI 2022); LBR (Lisý & Bowling, arXiv:1612.07547).
- Mechanism: seat-rotated duplicate deals under common random numbers cancel board/hole-deal luck (the dominant variance); AIVAT reuses the all-cards critic as a provably-unbiased control-variate baseline; the exploiter/LBR win-rate is the acceptance gate.
- Disruption: incremental (tooling). Expected gain: medium (enabling). Impl cost: medium.
- Axiom fit: clean (read-only; AIVAT baseline is the *learned* critic, not a hand-crafted equity — objective by construction).
- Guardrails (from the finding + its verify pass): **freeze the baseline before drawing eval hands** — a data-tuned baseline can be adversarially made to "prove" a player both won and lost (arXiv:2605.14261); inverse-variance-weight for ~24% more stderr; SHA-snapshot the baseline into the eval report (mirror the promote-flow SHA discipline). **Honesty note:** AIVAT as an *eval metric* is the clean win; the separate finding to fold AIVAT into the *training advantage* is **REFUTED** — its unbiasedness needs a fixed tabular strategy with per-infoset reach the NN self-play loop doesn't have, and the critic returns V for one sampled opp-hand, not the reach-weighted range expectation the correction requires.
- Applies to v5: extend `scripts/exploitability.py`; the concrete lever the design didn't spell out is "reuse the all-cards critic AS the AIVAT baseline." Build FIRST.

**2. Per-node purity & face-up-sizing exploitability metrics (the objective-function instruments)**
One-liner: three scalars on a frozen, versioned node bank — bet-size↔scoop-equity MI, gate-entropy on solver-pure nodes, bluff-catch call-frequency error — so "is v6 better on the thing we care about" is graded, not vibes.
- Source: recalled/composition — LBR range-read + MI/entropy estimation + the design's own critique definitions.
- Disruption: incremental. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: medium. Axiom fit: clean (engine counts + model probs; no subjective feature).
- Applies to v5: `scripts/probe_critiques.py` + a checked-in node bank (deterministic like the /ranges tab); reuse `check_sizing_dist.py`. This is what makes "promote v6?" correspond to the three critiques rather than raw bb/hand. Highest-leverage cheap item after #1.

**3. Pre-activation LayerNorm on the critic torso + weight-decay-to-init** — *Top 5 #4. PLAUSIBLE/medium, apply to critic first, pair with a shrink-perturb companion (LN-solo can hurt generalization), gate on the exploitability probe.*
- Source: verified-real — Nauman et al. NeurIPS 2024 (arXiv:2405.19153); Lyle et al. NaP (arXiv:2407.01800); plasticity survey (arXiv:2411.04832).
- Impl note: the actual block is single-Linear `x + ReLU(Linear(x))` (network.py:80-90), so the honest edit is `x + ReLU(Linear(LayerNorm(x)))`. Not function-preserving → fresh stem; gate behind `--torso-norm` sniffed from `torso.*.norm.weight`. Weight-decay-to-init is the required companion (normalization silently decays effective LR as weight-norm grows).

**4. PFSP / quality-score opponent sampling** — *Top 5 #5. Medium/low, clean, training-loop-only. Two variants (PFSP `f_hard` curve vs OpenAI-Five scalar-quality softmax with 20% history floor) — ship one; keep a high uniform floor for the intentional 2–6-seat coverage.*

**5. PopArt adaptive value normalization on the critic**
One-liner: normalize V/Q regression targets with running mean/std (output-preserving rescale) so the critic learns stably despite the wide bb-scale reward range across 2–6 seats and 20–250bb stacks.
- Source: verified-real — van Hasselt et al. PopArt (NeurIPS 2016, arXiv:1602.07714); MAPPO ("value normalization often helps, never hurts").
- Mechanism: decouples optimization scale from reward scale; especially synergistic with the VRPO Q flip (which needs a well-calibrated Q) and makes `value_clip` scale-free.
- Disruption: moderate. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: medium.
- Axiom fit: clean (critic training-only, never served; buffers init to identity = function-preserving no-op on a v5 critic). **Critical serve concern:** the (μ,σ) buffers MUST persist and apply identically if the *display* head adopts it, or the UI value shifts — mirror the ε-floor "serving must match training" lesson. Re-express `value_clip` in normalized units.

**6. Permutation-invariant (Deep-Sets) opponent-hole pooling in the critic**
One-liner: pool the 5 opponent slots through a shared encoder + symmetric aggregation instead of a fixed 5×52 concat, giving the all-cards critic exact seat-permutation invariance and seat-count-agnostic behavior.
- Source: verified-real — Deep Sets (arXiv:1703.06114); Set Transformer (arXiv:1810.00825).
- Mechanism: opponents are exchangeable given their holes; the concat forces the net to learn that invariance and handle 2–6 seats via zero-pad. A cleaner CTDE advantage helps #1/#2.
- Disruption: moderate. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: medium.
- Axiom fit: clean (critic-only, training-only → NO serve-path parity impact; safest structural change). Applies to v5: mask-pool over live seats so 2- and 6-seat states have no learned seat-count artifact.

**7. Minibatch-COUNT scaling (not batch-size/LR) as the large-batch stability knob**
One-liner: when the rollout grows, add MORE minibatch steps at *fixed* minibatch-size and LR — the opposite of letting minibatch size grow with the rollout.
- Source: verified-real — "Preventing Learning Stagnation in PPO by Scaling to 1M Parallel Envs" (arXiv:2603.06009); McCandlish et al. (arXiv:1812.06162).
- Mechanism: fixed minibatches + growing batch makes the outer step too large vs the now-lower noise → thrashing/plateau. Fixing minibatch size and raising the count lowers both step and noise.
- Disruption: incremental. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: low.
- Axiom fit: clean (loop accounting; no obs/warm-start impact). **Tension with the current VRAM-calibrated "KEEP 16 minibatches" rule** — expose as a mode and A/B, watching approx_kl + tier F/T/R.

**8. Muon optimizer on the residual torsos (hidden matrices only)** — *medium/medium, clean. Orthogonalized-momentum updates on the wide 2048/1536 Linear blocks equalize the update spectrum → fewer large-singular-direction jumps (a gate-collapse trigger); heads/embeddings/scalars stay AdamW (the boundary already exists). Caveat: the `kl_hard` rollback path (which snapshots Adam moments) must be generalized to snapshot Muon momentum, or it silently corrupts on rollback.*

**9. Grad checkpointing + bf16-with-fp32-logits** — *low/clean (checkpointing: identical math, memory-for-compute) and low/tension (bf16: obs-level parity preserved but step-level reproducibility lost under autocast — keep all dist/KL/advantage/value reductions fp32). Both are pure enablers of the larger batch the noise-scale analysis wants; value is contingent on the 90 GiB ceiling being the binding constraint.*

**REFUTED / cut:**
- **Ego-conditioned objective-outcome aux heads (SkyNet-style winner/rank prediction) — REFUTED (false premise + false cost).** Real source (Skyjo/MuZero +194 Elo), but (a) the finding's whole mechanism — "z must *discover* the joint scoop structure from per-board marginals" — is **false against the code**: the joint (scoop/quarter/hero/opp) block is already a direct actor INPUT at offset 978, so an aux head predicting it regresses a near-deterministic function of existing features (redundant); (b) the "labels already computed" claim is **false**: `double_board_payout` (verified, double_board.rs:17-72) returns a *merged scalar* per seat — per-board (share_A, share_B) labels need new Rust plumbing and a non-trivial side-pot split definition, so cost is not "low." ## Departures (bigger rebuilds)

Paradigm shifts — inference-time search, learned world models, opponent-mixture conditioning. Highest ceilings, highest cost, and every search variant here collides with the same wall: PLO5 double-board public belief states span ~1.4M holdings/player and multiway voids two-player subgame theory.

**1. SePoT: look-ahead search via a policy-TRANSFORMATION value critic (the lightest viable search)**
One-liner: train one extra critic that predicts the value of the policy under a few deterministic transforms (purify-gate, shift-anchor-up/down), then pick the transform that maximizes value at the served node — a cheap 1-ply "should I be purer / bet bigger here?" correction with NO belief-state enumeration.
- Source: verified-real — Kubicek, Burch & Lisý (SePoT), IJCAI 2024, arXiv:2312.15220.
- Mechanism: maps ~1:1 onto v5's staged Q infrastructure (the transformation-value critic is the dueling head one step further); reuses the exact q-aux training harness, dodges the 1.4M-holding wall.
- Disruption: moderate–departure. Expected gain: medium. Impl cost: high.
- Axiom fit: clean (additive zero-init head → function-preserving; objective leaf values; serve-side search). Applies to v5: extend `CentralCritic` so a sibling head predicts T policy-transform values; serve the 1-ply search in `ui/ranges.py`/a `/resolve` endpoint. **The middle path the design named ("invest in eval, not search") but didn't cost out** — cheaper than full re-solve, real test-time refinement, hits #1/#2/#3.

**2. Component-conditional refine head**
One-liner: condition the within-anchor Beta on which mixture component fired, so a "33% block" and a "thin value" component sharing an anchor carve different off-grid sizes.
- Source: recalled — MDN per-component heads (Bishop 1994); the documented v6 ceiling.
- Disruption: departure. Expected gain: low. Impl cost: high.
- Axiom fit: **tension** — v5 deliberately stores NO component index so the marginal-only sampling equivalence and exact closed-form marginal entropy hold; conditioning forces storing the component in the buffer (a real ActOut/PPO/parity change) OR a responsibility-weighted mixture Beta (heavier math). Warm-start preserved if new slots init to copies of the shared head. Only pursue if the size probe shows sub-anchor leakage.

**3. Larger/adaptive mixture-K (component birth-death)** — *incremental/low. Bumping K to 5–7 with the ε-floor is a one-line config + fresh stem (checkpoint shape changes; `mix_weight_floor < 1/K` may force lowering the 0.03 floor). Warm-start: copy the 3 components, tiny-init the new ones. Cheap, but second-order vs the magnet/Q levers.*

**4. Opponent-mixture-conditioned policy (NeuPL / Simplex-NeuPL)** — *departure/medium/high, tension. Conditioning the actor on the live seat-count (clean objective fact) is the axiom-safe half and directly helps multiway sizing/gating balance (#1/#2); conditioning on the pool-MIXTURE weights (a training-distribution descriptor) edges the objective-info axiom and must be pinned to a canonical value at serve. Changes OBS_DIM (tail append, zero-pad warm-start) + rollout threading.*

**5. Distributional (categorical/quantile) critic + two-hot/HL-Gauss value head** — *moderate/medium and incremental/medium respectively. HL-Gauss classification value loss (arXiv:2403.03950) is the single best-evidenced value-training upgrade for large nets and a low-footprint drop-in (V=E[bins], function-preserving init); the full distributional critic unlocks σ(s)-adaptive advantage clipping ("Moments Matter") and a distributional Q. **Honest caveat:** the classification/distributional edge shrinks under high environment stochasticity, and this reward is savagely high-variance — A/B the cheap two-hot head first to isolate the cross-entropy benefit. Risk-sensitive/CVaR objectives a distributional critic also unlocks are a **CONFLICT** for a GTO tool (they move off the EV equilibrium) — optional non-GTO study mode only.*

**REFUTED / cut (the search-that-needs-a-subsystem-you-don't-have cluster):**
- **Inference-time equilibrium re-solve / ReBeL-PoG-style subgame search — REFUTED (won't transfer as scoped, cost understated).** Real (ReBeL arXiv:2007.13544; Player of Games arXiv:2112.03178) and the study-tool axiom permits seconds/decision. **But ReBeL/PoG operate on PUBLIC BELIEF STATES with a RANGE-input value function; the v5 critic is not that** — `_critic_values` takes ONE concrete opponent-hole realization and returns a scalar (verified rollout.py:156), and the "true EV" only gets those cards at review/terminal. The literal proposal ("evaluate each action with the all-cards Q under MMD-damped rounds") is not executable at a live node without opponent-range reconstruction; the "depth-1 first cut" silently drops the load-bearing mechanism, leaving either a combinatorial marginalization over ~1.5M flop combos/opponent or a plain Q-greedy re-score with no equilibrium guarantee (already staged as VRPO). Prerequisite = a PBS/range-input value net + range-reconstruction module that deliberately don't exist. Standalone research project, not a v6 head/obs/training change.
- **LAMIR abstracted-public-state re-solve ("solver" button) — REFUTED (multiway + missing learned-abstraction subsystem).** Real (arXiv:2510.05048) and honestly summarized (beats R-NaD on II-Goofspiel), but (a) 2p0s-only — its continual-resolving safety and CFV bounds are 2p0s theorems, and bomb pots are natively multiway (the "HU/one-board-locked node" retreat is a thin rare slice, not the operating regime); (b) its tractability escape hatch is a *learned MuZero-style representation/dynamics/abstraction* the project doesn't have — you can't bolt CFR+ onto a PPO actor+critic, and its leaf values come from a learned vθ over the abstraction, not an external critic; (c) seeding leaf CFVs from the state-value critic has no re-solving correctness story and no ground-truth oracle to verify against (no product solves double-board bomb pots). High-ceiling but a standalone subsystem.
- **Safe subgame solving + sequential-equilibrium gadget refinement — kept as a DEPENDENCY, not a standalone item.** Real (Brown-Sandholm NeurIPS 2017; arXiv:2601.17131) and it's the *correctness governor* on any re-solve (naive gadgets have infinitely many equilibria equal in the gadget but wildly different in the full game — the difference between a safe study aid and a confidently-wrong one). Zero standalone gain; folds into #1/SePoT *if* built.

## Serving-side & schedule quick-wins (near-free, cross-cutting)

Small, low-risk changes that only touch what's SERVED or scheduled — no training-dynamics risk:
- **Serve the running (time-)average policy, not the last iterate.** In 2p0s self-play the last iterate provably cycles around Nash while the arithmetic average converges; the average is the least-exploitable servable artifact. Accumulate an arithmetic (windowed) mean of actor params alongside `model_ema`, add `PLO5BP_SERVE_AVG` (mirror the EMA-serving fallback). Purely additive checkpoint key, function-preserving, cheapest experiment in the whole catalog (multiway caveat: the guarantee is 2p0s, but "smoother, less exploitable readout" is what a study tool wants regardless).
- **WSD / decay-branch-on-promote.** Cosine needs a known horizon these open-ended pod runs don't have; keep constant LR on the main branch and either serve the EMA or run a short LR→0 decay branch *when promoting to the UI* for a smoother, less-exploitable served file. Formalizes what EMA-serving already half-does; the decay branch writes a separate `stub.pt` and never touches the training lineage.
- **SPRT accept/reject for autonomous promotion.** Wrap the duplicate/AIVAT eval in a Wald sequential likelihood-ratio test (the Stockfish/Fishtest standard) so eval STOPS as soon as it can conclude "v6 better / not," with fixed error bounds and no peeking penalty. ~30 lines; gates the "looks good → promote" flow statistically. Pairs with inverse-variance weighting.
- **Non-transitivity guard on Elo.** A single v6-beats-v5 bb/hand can be a rock-paper-scissors cycle; over the PSRO payoff matrix compute the transitive (HodgeRank/Elo-decomposition) component + a cycle count and report v6's improvement on the *transitive* axis. Guardrail against the autonomous promote flow chasing cycles.

## Caveats

- **Web-verified vs recalled.** Findings tagged `verified-real` had their source papers fetched/confirmed and, for the CONFIRMED/REFUTED verdicts, the load-bearing claims checked against both the paper and the repo code (I re-verified in-repo the joint-outcome obs block at offset 978, the `double_board_payout` merged-scalar return, the `nlh_opp_outcome`/PLO opp-outcome "current-rank, no runout" convention, the zero-init `adv_head`/`q_idx` staging, the `_kl_to_reference` gate-KL-from-logits, and vFive1's live fixed `--kl-anchor-coef 0.05`). The `recalled` items (size-balance regularizer, component-outcome aux, purified-reference composition) are design syntheses with no single citation — treat them as proposals, not results.
- **Two verdicts override scout optimism the reader should not miss:** (1) the entire NashPG/R-NaD/GARIP "iterated-magnet" family is proven **two-player-zero-sum only** and its guarantee is *void in native-multiway bomb pots* — cheap and worth a heads-up-NLH-stem A/B, but NOT the high-gain PLO5 convergence fix the sources sell; (2) the JOINT-outcome obs feature and the ego-outcome aux head both **failed on code facts** (already-shipped / labels-don't-exist), not on theory.
- **Magnitudes don't transfer 1:1.** VRPO's +33±19 mBB/h is single-board heads-up ("not a decisive win" per its authors); HL-Gauss/distributional gains shrink under this reward's extreme variance; every "lower exploitability" number is from small or single-board games. Use them as *direction*, gated by the eval stack.
- **This is a research catalog, not a validated benchmark.** Nothing here is A/B'd on PLO5 double-board. The prerequisite that makes any of it measurable — the duplicate/CRN + AIVAT + probe eval stack (Top-5 #3) — should be built first; then the honest sequence is VRPO flip → value-clip/critic rebalance → reverse-KL magnet + purified reference / gate purification → PFSP → LayerNorm(+companion) on the critic, each arbitrated by the three per-critique scalars.
