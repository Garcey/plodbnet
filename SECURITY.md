# Security

WrapGTO (wrapgto.com) handles sign-ins, subscriptions and private home games. If you
find a vulnerability, please report it privately:

- **Email:** themilesgarcia@icloud.com — subject "WrapGTO security"
- Please do not open a public issue, and do not access other people's accounts or
  data beyond what is needed to show the problem.

You will get an answer within a few days. Thank you for helping keep the site safe.
