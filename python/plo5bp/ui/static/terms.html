5. Home games

A ledger, not a cashier. Chips at a home-game table stand for amounts the players agree on among themselves. WrapGTO records buy-ins, top-ups and cash-outs and shows who owes whom, but no money is paid to, held by, or sent through WrapGTO, and we take no rake or fee. Settling up happens entirely between players, outside the site. WrapGTO is not a party to those arrangements and does not guarantee that anyone pays. Playing for money may be restricted where you live: it's your responsibility to follow your local law.

Clubs and tables. A club's owner and admins decide who joins and may remove members; a table's host sets its rules (stakes, clock, who may sit, whether grades are shown) and may remove players from it.

What other players see. The people at your tables, and the members of your clubs, see your display name and picture, your chat, and your actions at the table. Inside a club they can also see your results, the ledger, your stats and your hand histories. Your hole cards are shown to others only when the table's rules reveal them (for example at showdown, or when you choose to show), and grades follow the table's setting.

The shuffle. Each deal is shuffled by our server and then cut using a random number from every participating player's device. After a hand, anyone who took part can check that the deal was not changed after their device contributed. The server still deals the cards, so it cannot prove that nobody at WrapGTO could see them; if that matters to you, don't play.

Fair play. No collusion, no bots or scripts, no chip dumping, no using the study tools during a home-game hand, and no abuse of other players. Hosts, club owners and we may remove anyone who breaks these rules.
